## A little help can go a long way

Sahayogi Nepal gives people a simple way to ask for support or offer resources. Volunteers can step in where they are needed, while local coordinators help each request find its way to the right hands.

Made during a 48-hour hackathon, the project is a small first step toward making community support easier to find and easier to give.
