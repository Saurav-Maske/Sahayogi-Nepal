SAHAYOGI NEPAL VOLUNTEER TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. PURPOSE
Sahayogi Nepal connects people seeking support with volunteers and community resources. The app helps coordinate requests and offers; it does not guarantee that every request will be matched or fulfilled.

2. VOLUNTEER ELIGIBILITY AND INFORMATION
You agree to provide accurate, current information during registration. You must be legally able to perform the volunteer activities you select. If you select Transport Manpower or Health Worker, you must provide the requested supporting license and keep it valid. Sahayogi Nepal may ask you to verify information or may restrict access when information is incomplete, misleading, or cannot be verified.

3. VOLUNTEER CONDUCT
You agree to act respectfully, safely, and lawfully; follow applicable instructions and safety guidance; treat people receiving support with dignity; protect private information; and perform only tasks for which you are qualified. Do not discriminate, harass, exploit, or request payment or personal benefit in exchange for volunteer help.

4. SAFETY AND EMERGENCIES
Use your judgment and do not put yourself or others at unreasonable risk. Do not represent yourself as a licensed professional unless you hold the required current credentials. The app is not an emergency service. For immediate danger or urgent medical emergencies, contact the appropriate local emergency services.

5. LOCATION AND COMMUNICATION
If you use location features, your device may share location information to help coordinate nearby support. Keep contact details current and use the app only for coordination related to community support. Avoid sharing sensitive personal information unless necessary for a request.

6. DOCUMENTS AND PRIVACY
Registration documents and personal information should be used only for volunteer registration, eligibility checks, and coordination of support. Access should be limited to people who need the information for these purposes. Do not upload documents belonging to another person without their permission. You may contact Sahayogi Nepal to request correction or removal of information, subject to applicable record-keeping requirements.

7. REQUESTS AND MATCHING
Requests, availability, and status updates may be incomplete or delayed. Matching depends on available volunteers, resources, distance, and circumstances. Confirm important details directly with the relevant people before taking action.

8. ACCOUNT ACCESS
Keep your account credentials private and notify Sahayogi Nepal if you believe your account has been used without authorization. Sahayogi Nepal may suspend or end access for a serious or repeated violation of these terms, safety concerns, or misuse of the service.

9. LIMITATION
To the extent permitted by law, Sahayogi Nepal is not responsible for losses arising from inaccurate information supplied by users, unavailable volunteers or resources, communication failures, or actions taken by users outside the app. Nothing in these terms excludes responsibility that cannot legally be excluded.

10. CHANGES AND CONTACT
These terms may be updated as the service changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised terms. For questions, corrections, or privacy requests, contact the Sahayogi Nepal team through the contact channel provided in the app.

By registering as a volunteer, you confirm that you have read and agree to these terms.
